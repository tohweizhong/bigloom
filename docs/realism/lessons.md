# Realism loop lessons for the proposer

The proposer reads this file. It holds train results and lessons only. It holds no holdout seed, no
holdout number, and no holdout passage. The orchestrator updates it after each round, under the rules
in [`docs/self-improvement.md`](../self-improvement.md).

## What the metrics can and cannot show

- `commercial_per_k` counts the two-word phrases `gross profit`, `gross margin`, `operating margin`,
  `EBITDA`, and `sales volume`. The single word `margin` does not count.
- Loop v2 plants `gross surplus` and `surplus rate` in a public-sector pack. These phrases do not
  count as commercial.
- Loop v2 does not score the raw `unique_sentences` count. A count grows with file length. Receipts
  still report it.
- Most repeated sentences in the champion come from the PDF review record table and the cover
  abstract. The writer policy does not control them. A writer rule cannot lower
  `within_file_repeat` by a useful amount.

## Trade-offs seen in loop v1 (train numbers)

- **Shorter sentences add commercial phrases.** Rules for short sentences raised `unique_sentences`.
  Writers then named `gross profit` once in each new paragraph. Train `commercial_per_k` rose from 2.26
  to 4.78 (Round 5), 4.09 (Round 8), 3.48 (Round 14), and 6.47 (Round 15).
- **Fewer commercial phrases make long sentences.** Rules that cut commercial phrases made writers
  join facts into compound sentences of 25 to 30 words. Train `unique_sentences` fell from 48.8 to
  35.3 (Round 4), 31.2 (Round 11), and 38.5 (Round 13).
- **Fewer paragraphs hurt the PPTX in v1.** Each paragraph becomes one slide bullet. With fewer
  bullets, slide footers took a larger share of the openers. Grader v2 skips slide footers.
- **The word `margin` affects retrieval.** If the PDF and PPTX prose drop the word `margin`, the
  retrieval gate can fail for the DOCX chart query (Rounds 9, 10, 12). Keep the word `margin` in the
  prose where the facts support it.
- **Named sections fail.** Rules that name sections by title failed, because the section titles
  differ between documents (Round 11).

## Judge clues (masked judges, train passages)

The masked judges told real from synthetic in every pair after Rounds 2 and 11. Their clues:

- Synthetic side: a polished narrative tone, and capital letters in the middle of a sentence where a
  fact is joined into the text.
- Real side: PDF extraction marks, real external standards, and `.gov.sg` domains. The writer policy
  cannot change these real-side clues.

- **rs016 champion baseline (23 Oct 20:06 SGT, train passages, 3 fresh judges): 20, 20, and 20 of
  20 correct.** Commercial phrases did not appear as a clue. The judges named these clues:
  - The synthetic side comes from only two document families: a budget utilisation report (or
    Exco update) and an incident review about a stale code mapping.
  - The synthetic prose is polished and gives advice to a named role ("the cluster finance
    partner"). The incident review tells a neat story about a wrong theory, then closes on control
    failure and ownership.
  - One table repeats the same cell across all four columns.
  - The real side is varied operational text: design-standard controls, step guides, a security
    profile, threat tables, and product pages, with PDF extraction noise.

- **rescore-v2-017 (23 Oct 20:50 SGT, train passages, 3 fresh judges): 20, 20, and 20 of 20
  correct.** 15 of the 20 synthetic passages came from the PDF incident review, because the longest

  - One invented incident review (stale code mapping, SKU counts, manual override) and one finance

## Writer behaviour

- One writer run for each seed is noisy. Under the same policy, two writers wrote 0 and 32 commercial
  phrases.
- Some writers wrote half the target length (Rounds 7 and 11). A length rule can help, but it also
  changed other metrics (Round 8).

### Lessons from Loop v2 rounds (train only)

- **`judge_error` (`0.0` vs `0.5` target) is the only metric with room after `train-031r.json`, and clean-side `top_opener_share <= 0.071` no longer triggers `worsened`.** After the Level 2 fix in `7223e7c`, `distances()` computes one-sided defect distances `max(0.0, vector[k] - real[k])`, so any candidate with `top_opener_share <= 0.081` has zero worsened penalty on `top_opener_share`. In addition, `pdf_add_picture` places the figure page at the end of the PDF (`P11` is no longer split by `Figure: ...`) and `filtered_longform()` uses short `< 6`-word slide titles (`P04` and `P08` no longer repeat the opening prose clause). Every proposal from Round 32 onward should target `judge_error` by replacing `"say what they mean"` in `QUALITY` with a rule that eliminates the repetitive incident/variance essay commentary and writes direct, concrete public-sector technical specifications and control requirements while keeping total words above the 80% length guard (at least 95% of `target_words`).
- **What train judges cite in `pairs.md` on `r029t` and `r030t`.** With all PDF, DOCX, and PPTX scaffolding removed by the Level 2 filter (`081df3c`), the train judges in Rounds 29 and 30 identified the synthetic passages by: (1) inline figure citations (`Figure: System error rate (%) and manual override count during the valuation window`, `Figure: Inventory valuation reconciliation variance by division ($k)`), (2) identical repeated `{{fact:FACT-ID}}` text clauses across multiple passages (`ERP logs show no interruption during the valuation window`, `manual agency code mapping override applied to complete valuation for the period`), and (3) dense narrative paragraphs about the same two storylines (`inventory valuation service` / `product hierarchy sync service` and `Exco update | 2026-03` financial variances) compared with the concise, imperative public-sector guidance and service standards in the real GovTech Singapore passages.

- **Level 2 scaffold filter after Round 28 (`081df3c`, `train-028r.json`).** Before `081df3c`, 10 of the 20 synthetic passages in `pairs.md` contained WorldLoom renderer scaffolding (`Document control`, `Revision history`, `Review record`, `Approval and sign-off form`, `Related documents`, mid-sentence PDF running headers, and truncated PPTX bullets), so even when Rounds 26–28 improved the writer prose, half of the passages in `pairs.md` still exposed synthetic metadata. Now `longform_filter.py` strips all of that scaffolding, so all 20 pairs in `pairs.md` are pure writer prose. On `train-028r.json`, the only remaining clues cited by the train judges on the `round-002.md` champion are its reflective essay transitions (`"Put simply"`, `"Read together, these two facts mean"`, `"For a technology reader"`, `"Six months from now"`). Replacing `"say what they mean"` in `QUALITY` with a single rule that requires direct institutional compliance, parameter, and control specifications at full `target_words` length without reflective essay transitions, narrative detours, or reader advice now tests writer prose directly across all 20 pairs.

- **Do not delete `"Lead with the position, group related facts, say what they mean"` without replacement expansion.** When Round 17 replaced that clause with a ban on essay commentary, writers cut their section length to ~2550 words (`seed_2027`: 2631 and 2553 words vs 2815 threshold) and failed the 80% length guard and the `bl-002-pdf-5mb-image` retrieval gate. Keep the instruction to group related facts and explain their operational effect so sections reach `target_words` and preserve retrieval terms.
- **Placing text facts after a colon shortened `seed_2026` below the 80% length guard.** In Round 21, adding `"Place every capitalised text fact at the start of a sentence or after a colon."` passed the train build and retrieval gates with zero violations, but `seed_2026` wrote 2554 and 2377 words (below the 2787.2 threshold) because `narrate accept` checks sentence counts (`section_floor`), not `target_words`. Do not stack a colon rule with a second length rule in one round (that violates the single-rule requirement). Instead, test a single rule change that keeps prose at full length while removing essay commentary or reader-directed advice.
- **The word `store` or `stores` (even in `"data store"` or `"parameter stores"`) counts in `retail_per_k`.** In Round 17 (`retail_per_k` 0.705) and Round 18 (`retail_per_k` 0.453), writers used `store`/`stores` in technical prose (`data store`, `parameter stores`). Never use `store` or `stores` in prose; write `repository`, `register`, or `database`.
- **Expanding final sections dilutes BM25 on the planted forecast table page.** In Round 18, telling writers to `"Expand each point with concrete system workflows..."` lengthened the closing sections before the planted forecast table in `bl-001-docx-2mb` and `bl-002-pdf-2mb`, which diluted BM25 density on the trap passage and caused `bl-001-docx-2mb-image` and `bl-002-pdf-2mb-image` to fail `target_or_trap_not_in_top3`. Keep section lengths near `target_words` (around 95–100%, not 110–120%) and repeat the document title or business unit names (`Product & Engineering`, `Government Digital Transformation`) naturally in the prose so the document ranks in top 3.
- **Level 2 retrieval fix after Round 19.** After Round 19, a Level 2 fix deduplicated PPTX slide units in `qualify._reader_units` and added the chart caption to DOCX and PDF trap tables in `FormatHandler.canary_ir`. With this fix in place, Round 19's train build (`r019t`) passes all retrieval gates and improves `within_file_repeat` from 0.024 to 0.013 with zero `commercial_per_k` and zero `retail_per_k`. Round 20 can therefore re-test Round 19's rule change (or a refined version of it) under the fixed retrieval gate.
- **Level 2 DOCX picture placement fix after Round 22 (`05fdb96`, `train-022r.json`).** After Round 22, a Level 2 fix updated `docx_add_picture` to match the evidence table from the end of `doc.tables` (skipping the cover-page table of contents) and to include the figure caption row in the evidence table. On the rebuilt code (`05fdb96`), the champion train vector is `within_file_repeat` 0.024, `top_opener_share` 0.063, `commercial_per_k` 0.0, and `retail_per_k` 0.0, and Round 22's rule change (`"Lead with the position, group related facts, and explain their operational effect on programme delivery and budgets without essay commentary or reader advice."`) passes all retrieval gates while improving train `within_file_repeat` from 0.024 to 0.013 with `commercial_per_k` 0.0.
- **Why `seed_2026` fell short of the 80% length guard in Rounds 21, 22 (attempt 1), and 23, and how sentence count controls `within_file_repeat`.** Both `seed_2026` and `seed_2027` have 17 requests whose `target_words` sum to 3,620 words (90% is 3,258 words, above the 2,787.2 and 2,814.4 length guards). In Round 24, replacing `"say what they mean"` with `"and explain their operational effect on programme delivery, schedules, and budgets in full paragraphs so every section reaches at least 90 percent of target_words without essay commentary or reader advice"` made both train seeds pass the length guard on attempt 1 (`seed_2026`: 3,377 words, `seed_2027`: 2,949 words) with zero `commercial_per_k` and zero `retail_per_k`. However, the Round 24 writers wrote 38-to-42-word compound sentences, so the two 2,350-word train PDFs had only 64 and 55 sentences (`within_file_repeat` 0.062 and 0.036, 6-file mean 0.016, delta 0.008 vs the 0.01 band). In contrast, Round 22 had 84 and 66 PDF sentences and reached `within_file_repeat` 0.013 (delta 0.011 > 0.01 band). Replacing `"say what they mean"` in `QUALITY` with `"and explain their operational effect on programme delivery, schedules, and budgets in sentences of 18 to 24 words so every section reaches at least 95 percent of target_words without essay commentary or reader advice"` is a single rule change that keeps total words above 3,300 while raising PDF sentence counts to 90–105 sentences and lowering train `within_file_repeat` to 0.010–0.012.
